Technologiefelder

Praktische industrielle Technologie, aufgebaut auf Engineering.

Tech Forge Collective verbindet industrielle Erfahrung, deterministisches Engineering, AI, Robotik, Automation und Software, um Werkzeuge zu bauen, denen Engineers vertrauen koennen. Diese Bereiche beschreiben Unternehmensfaehigkeiten und Forschungsrichtungen, nicht eine Liste oeffentlicher Produkte.

AI

Industrielle KI

Problembereich: Industrieteams brauchen AI, die Nachweise, Randbedingungen und Sicherheit respektiert.

Engineering-Wert: AI unterstuetzt Urteil, indem sie Kontext, Optionen, Risiken und fehlende Informationen sichtbar macht.

TFC-Ansatz: AI-Reasoning mit deterministischen Checks, Quellenqualitaet, menschlicher Aufsicht und rueckverfolgbaren Outputs verbinden.

RB

Robotik

Problembereich: Feldrobotik muss Bewegung, Sensorik, Energie, Kommunikation und Bedienersicherheit beherrschen.

Engineering-Wert: Robotiktechnologien koennen Inspektion, Monitoring und Automation in schwierige Umgebungen erweitern.

TFC-Ansatz: Robotik als Engineering-System behandeln: Steuerung, Reliability, Wissen, Software und menschliche Freigabe zusammen.

SW

Industriesoftware

Problembereich: Technical teams often work across fragile spreadsheets, disconnected files, and generic tools.

Engineering-Wert: Purpose-built software can make engineering work clearer, more repeatable, and easier to review.

TFC-Ansatz: Build focused systems around evidence, workflows, reports, permissions, data quality, and practical operations.

AU

Automation

Problembereich: Repeated engineering work is easy to perform inconsistently when knowledge stays informal.

Engineering-Wert: Automation turns repeatable work into controlled digital workflows with review points.

TFC-Ansatz: Automate the structure around work while keeping decisions, exceptions, and approvals visible.

EI

Engineering Intelligence

Problembereich: Engineering work depends on context, calculations, documents, decisions, and long-term knowledge.

Engineering-Wert: Intelligence becomes useful when it connects technical evidence to workflows and decisions.

TFC-Ansatz: Use AI for orchestration and reasoning, deterministic tools for calculations, and citations for review.

DE

Digital Engineering

Problembereich: Engineering delivery needs structured requirements, tasks, reports, readiness, and change history.

Engineering-Wert: Digital engineering systems help teams move from scattered activity to accountable execution.

TFC-Ansatz: Model projects, cases, reports, tasks, and lessons as durable engineering objects.

AR

Asset- und Reliability-Technologie

Problembereich: Asset decisions need maintenance knowledge, operating context, risk, and evidence over time.

Engineering-Wert: Reliability thinking improves how teams understand failures, readiness, and operational constraints.

TFC-Ansatz: Connect evidence, lessons, reports, and engineering reasoning so reliability knowledge compounds.

KG

Engineering-Wissenssysteme

Problembereich: Knowledge is often trapped in old projects, documents, conversations, and individual memory.

Engineering-Wert: Structured knowledge preserves standards, decisions, lessons, and capability across future work.

TFC-Ansatz: Build searchable knowledge structures with source quality, relationships, and human review.

Flagship-Beispiel

Wie die Bereiche in ProjecTile zusammenkommen

ProjecTile combines engineering intelligence, industrial software, automation, digital engineering, and engineering knowledge systems into one Engineering Operating System. It is the named public platform; other TFC work remains technology in development, research, or internal capability.
