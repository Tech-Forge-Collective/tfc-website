Engineering Intelligence

Beyond chat. Built for engineering.

ProjecTile is designed as an Engineering Intelligence Platform that helps technical teams connect knowledge, projects, specialists, reports, and decision-making into one focused workspace.

Explore ProjecTile

Engineering First

Most AI systems are designed for conversation.

ProjecTile is designed for engineering work.

Built around real project context.

It is being built around projects, documentation, technical reasoning, reports, engineering workflows, and long-term knowledge rather than isolated conversations.

Connected Knowledge

Engineering knowledge exists across drawings, reports, calculations, manuals, standards, and experience.

ProjecTile aims to connect these sources into a searchable engineering intelligence layer that supports real technical work.

Engineering Specialists

Instead of one general-purpose assistant, ProjecTile is designed around specialist engineering roles that collaborate using shared project context and engineering knowledge.

Human-Centred Intelligence

Engineers remain responsible for engineering decisions.

ProjecTile is intended to improve clarity, preserve knowledge, and accelerate technical work - not replace engineering judgement.

Built for Complex Projects

Designed for environments where documentation, engineering disciplines, automation, software, and operational knowledge must work together.
