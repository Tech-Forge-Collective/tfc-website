ProjecTile Roadmap

Construyendo un Engineering Operating System capacidad por capacidad.

Deze publieke roadmap toont alleen strategische richting. Geen datums, interne architectuur, providerinformatie of vertrouwelijke implementatieplannen.

Foco actual Claridad de plataforma central

Navigation, product separation, readiness evidence, and publication polish.

Senal de benchmark Mejorando

Website-readiness en productcommunicatie bewegen richting publieke reviewkwaliteit.

Alcance publico Estrategico

Geen releasedatums, vertrouwelijke stackdetails of interne projectnamen worden getoond.

01 Completado

Fundacion

Brandsysteem, statische websitebasis, SEO-baseline, repositorysplit en publieke identiteit.

  • TFC company story established.
  • ProjecTile positioned as flagship platform.
  • Public routes and metadata prepared.

Readiness context: foundation items are stable and reviewable.

02 En progreso

Engineering Intelligence

Turn engineering requests into structured work with specialists, deterministic tools, QA, and reporting.

  • Engineering Director workflow.
  • Specialist coordination.
  • Informes and deterministic calculations.

Readiness context: core workflow evidence exists; polish and validation continue.

03 En progreso

Knowledge Graph and Engineering Memory

Connect documents, reports, decisions, cases, knowledge packs, and Engineering DNA into durable memory.

  • Knowledge relationships.
  • Source quality scoring.
  • Engineering DNA promotion.

Readiness context: relationship and quality layers are active improvement areas.

04 Planificado

Engineering Learning

Use capability gaps and knowledge quality to recommend packs, projects, reports, and experiments.

  • Learning roadmaps.
  • Capability recommendations.
  • Confidence improvement actions.

Readiness context: planned public surface, with internal evidence growing.

05 Planificado

Founder Review

Harden the first public story, validate product language, review risks, and prepare feedback loops.

  • Product narrative review.
  • Accessibility and performance checks.
  • Public demo scope control.

Readiness context: awaiting final content and founder signoff.

06 Planificado

Public Demo

Release a focused public ProjecTile experience that explains value without exposing internal mechanics.

  • Controlled demo CTA.
  • Clear roadmap and updates.
  • Production SEO and route hygiene.

Readiness context: dependent on founder review and final demo boundaries.
