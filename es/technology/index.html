Líneas tecnológicas

Tecnología industrial práctica, construida sobre ingeniería.

Tech Forge Collective combineert industriele ervaring, deterministische engineering, AI, robotica, automatisering en software om tools te bouwen die engineers kunnen vertrouwen. Deze lijnen beschrijven bedrijfscapabilities en onderzoeksrichtingen, niet een lijst met publieke producten.

AI

IA industrial

Area del problema: Industriele teams hebben AI nodig die bewijs, beperkingen en veiligheid respecteert.

Valor de ingenieria: AI ondersteunt oordeel door context, opties, risico's en ontbrekende informatie zichtbaar te maken.

Enfoque TFC: Combineer AI-redenering met deterministische checks, bronkwaliteit, menselijk toezicht en traceerbare outputs.

RB

Robotica

Area del problema: Veldrobotica moet beweging, sensing, vermogen, communicatie en operatorveiligheid beheersen.

Valor de ingenieria: Roboticatechnologie kan inspectie, monitoring en automatisering uitbreiden naar moeilijke omgevingen.

Enfoque TFC: Behandel robotica als engineeringsysteem: besturing, betrouwbaarheid, kennis, software en menselijke goedkeuring samen.

SW

Software industrial

Area del problema: Technical teams often work across fragile spreadsheets, disconnected files, and generic tools.

Valor de ingenieria: Purpose-built software can make engineering work clearer, more repeatable, and easier to review.

Enfoque TFC: Build focused systems around evidence, workflows, reports, permissions, data quality, and practical operations.

AU

Automation

Area del problema: Repeated engineering work is easy to perform inconsistently when knowledge stays informal.

Valor de ingenieria: Automation turns repeatable work into controlled digital workflows with review points.

Enfoque TFC: Automate the structure around work while keeping decisions, exceptions, and approvals visible.

EI

Engineering Intelligence

Area del problema: Engineering work depends on context, calculations, documents, decisions, and long-term knowledge.

Valor de ingenieria: Intelligence becomes useful when it connects technical evidence to workflows and decisions.

Enfoque TFC: Use AI for orchestration and reasoning, deterministic tools for calculations, and citations for review.

DE

Ingenieria digital

Area del problema: Engineering delivery needs structured requirements, tasks, reports, readiness, and change history.

Valor de ingenieria: Digital engineering systems help teams move from scattered activity to accountable execution.

Enfoque TFC: Model projects, cases, reports, tasks, and lessons as durable engineering objects.

AR

Tecnologia de activos y fiabilidad

Area del problema: Asset decisions need maintenance knowledge, operating context, risk, and evidence over time.

Valor de ingenieria: Reliability thinking improves how teams understand failures, readiness, and operational constraints.

Enfoque TFC: Connect evidence, lessons, reports, and engineering reasoning so reliability knowledge compounds.

KG

Sistemas de conocimiento de ingenieria

Area del problema: Knowledge is often trapped in old projects, documents, conversations, and individual memory.

Valor de ingenieria: Structured knowledge preserves standards, decisions, lessons, and capability across future work.

Enfoque TFC: Build searchable knowledge structures with source quality, relationships, and human review.

Ejemplo flagship

Hoe de lijnen samenkomen in ProjecTile

ProjecTile combines engineering intelligence, industrial software, automation, digital engineering, and engineering knowledge systems into one Engineering Operating System. It is the named public platform; other TFC work remains technology in development, research, or internal capability.
