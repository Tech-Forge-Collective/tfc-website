Axes technologiques

Technologie industrielle pratique, construite sur l’ingénierie.

Tech Forge Collective combine experience industrielle, ingenierie deterministe, AI, robotique, automatisation et software pour construire des outils fiables pour les ingenieurs. Ces axes decrivent les capacites de l’entreprise et les directions de recherche, pas une liste de produits publics.

AI

IA industrielle

Zone de probleme : Les equipes industrielles ont besoin d’AI qui respecte preuves, contraintes et securite.

Valeur d’ingenierie : L’AI soutient le jugement en faisant apparaitre contexte, options, risques et informations manquantes.

Approche TFC : Associer le raisonnement AI a des controles deterministes, qualite des sources, supervision humaine et sorties tracables.

RB

Robotique

Zone de probleme : La robotique terrain doit gerer mouvement, capteurs, energie, communications et securite operateur.

Valeur d’ingenierie : Les technologies robotiques peuvent etendre inspection, surveillance et automatisation aux environnements difficiles.

Approche TFC : Traiter la robotique comme un systeme d’ingenierie: controles, reliability, connaissance, software et approbation humaine ensemble.

SW

Software industriel

Zone de probleme : Technical teams often work across fragile spreadsheets, disconnected files, and generic tools.

Valeur d’ingenierie : Purpose-built software can make engineering work clearer, more repeatable, and easier to review.

Approche TFC : Build focused systems around evidence, workflows, reports, permissions, data quality, and practical operations.

AU

Automation

Zone de probleme : Repeated engineering work is easy to perform inconsistently when knowledge stays informal.

Valeur d’ingenierie : Automation turns repeatable work into controlled digital workflows with review points.

Approche TFC : Automate the structure around work while keeping decisions, exceptions, and approvals visible.

EI

Engineering Intelligence

Zone de probleme : Engineering work depends on context, calculations, documents, decisions, and long-term knowledge.

Valeur d’ingenierie : Intelligence becomes useful when it connects technical evidence to workflows and decisions.

Approche TFC : Use AI for orchestration and reasoning, deterministic tools for calculations, and citations for review.

DE

Ingenierie numerique

Zone de probleme : Engineering delivery needs structured requirements, tasks, reports, readiness, and change history.

Valeur d’ingenierie : Digital engineering systems help teams move from scattered activity to accountable execution.

Approche TFC : Model projects, cases, reports, tasks, and lessons as durable engineering objects.

AR

Technologie actifs et fiabilite

Zone de probleme : Asset decisions need maintenance knowledge, operating context, risk, and evidence over time.

Valeur d’ingenierie : Reliability thinking improves how teams understand failures, readiness, and operational constraints.

Approche TFC : Connect evidence, lessons, reports, and engineering reasoning so reliability knowledge compounds.

KG

Systemes de connaissance d?ingenierie

Zone de probleme : Knowledge is often trapped in old projects, documents, conversations, and individual memory.

Valeur d’ingenierie : Structured knowledge preserves standards, decisions, lessons, and capability across future work.

Approche TFC : Build searchable knowledge structures with source quality, relationships, and human review.

Exemple flagship

Comment les axes se rejoignent dans ProjecTile

ProjecTile combines engineering intelligence, industrial software, automation, digital engineering, and engineering knowledge systems into one Engineering Operating System. It is the named public platform; other TFC work remains technology in development, research, or internal capability.
