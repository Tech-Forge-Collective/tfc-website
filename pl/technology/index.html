Obszary technologii

Praktyczna technologia przemysłowa oparta na inżynierii.

Tech Forge Collective laczy doswiadczenie przemyslowe, deterministyczna inzynierie, AI, robotyke, automatyzacje i software, aby budowac narzedzia godne zaufania inzynierow. Te obszary opisuja capability firmy i kierunki badan, a nie liste publicznych produktow.

AI

AI przemyslowa

Obszar problemu: Zespoly przemyslowe potrzebuja AI, ktora respektuje dowody, ograniczenia i bezpieczenstwo.

Wartosc inzynierska: AI wspiera osad, ujawniajac kontekst, opcje, ryzyka i brakujace informacje.

Podejscie TFC: Lacz rozumowanie AI z deterministycznymi kontrolami, jakoscia zrodel, nadzorem czlowieka i traceable wynikami.

RB

Robotyka

Obszar problemu: Robotyka terenowa musi obslugiwac ruch, sensoryke, zasilanie, komunikacje i bezpieczenstwo operatora.

Wartosc inzynierska: Technologie robotyczne moga rozszerzac inspekcje, monitoring i automatyzacje na trudne srodowiska.

Podejscie TFC: Traktuj robotyke jako system inzynierski: sterowanie, reliability, wiedza, software i akceptacja czlowieka razem.

SW

Software przemyslowy

Obszar problemu: Technical teams often work across fragile spreadsheets, disconnected files, and generic tools.

Wartosc inzynierska: Purpose-built software can make engineering work clearer, more repeatable, and easier to review.

Podejscie TFC: Build focused systems around evidence, workflows, reports, permissions, data quality, and practical operations.

AU

Automation

Obszar problemu: Repeated engineering work is easy to perform inconsistently when knowledge stays informal.

Wartosc inzynierska: Automation turns repeatable work into controlled digital workflows with review points.

Podejscie TFC: Automate the structure around work while keeping decisions, exceptions, and approvals visible.

EI

Engineering Intelligence

Obszar problemu: Engineering work depends on context, calculations, documents, decisions, and long-term knowledge.

Wartosc inzynierska: Intelligence becomes useful when it connects technical evidence to workflows and decisions.

Podejscie TFC: Use AI for orchestration and reasoning, deterministic tools for calculations, and citations for review.

DE

Inzynieria cyfrowa

Obszar problemu: Engineering delivery needs structured requirements, tasks, reports, readiness, and change history.

Wartosc inzynierska: Digital engineering systems help teams move from scattered activity to accountable execution.

Podejscie TFC: Model projects, cases, reports, tasks, and lessons as durable engineering objects.

AR

Technologia asset i reliability

Obszar problemu: Asset decisions need maintenance knowledge, operating context, risk, and evidence over time.

Wartosc inzynierska: Reliability thinking improves how teams understand failures, readiness, and operational constraints.

Podejscie TFC: Connect evidence, lessons, reports, and engineering reasoning so reliability knowledge compounds.

KG

Inzynierskie systemy wiedzy

Obszar problemu: Knowledge is often trapped in old projects, documents, conversations, and individual memory.

Wartosc inzynierska: Structured knowledge preserves standards, decisions, lessons, and capability across future work.

Podejscie TFC: Build searchable knowledge structures with source quality, relationships, and human review.

Przyklad flagship

Jak obszary lacza sie w ProjecTile

ProjecTile combines engineering intelligence, industrial software, automation, digital engineering, and engineering knowledge systems into one Engineering Operating System. It is the named public platform; other TFC work remains technology in development, research, or internal capability.
