FAQ

Roadmap notes.

Why are there no release dates?

The roadmap is intentionally strategic. Priorities can change as ProjecTile evolves and as engineering needs become clearer.

Why are features marked as vision?

Vision items describe long-term direction. They are included to show intent, not to promise a fixed delivery scope.
