Technologielijnen

Praktische industriële technologie, gebouwd op engineering.

Tech Forge Collective combineert industriele ervaring, deterministische engineering, AI, robotica, automatisering en software om tools te bouwen die engineers kunnen vertrouwen. Deze lijnen beschrijven bedrijfscapabilities en onderzoeksrichtingen, niet een lijst met publieke producten.

AI

Industriele AI

Probleemgebied: Industriele teams hebben AI nodig die bewijs, beperkingen en veiligheid respecteert.

Engineeringwaarde: AI ondersteunt oordeel door context, opties, risico's en ontbrekende informatie zichtbaar te maken.

TFC-aanpak: Combineer AI-redenering met deterministische checks, bronkwaliteit, menselijk toezicht en traceerbare outputs.

RB

Robotica

Probleemgebied: Veldrobotica moet beweging, sensing, vermogen, communicatie en operatorveiligheid beheersen.

Engineeringwaarde: Roboticatechnologie kan inspectie, monitoring en automatisering uitbreiden naar moeilijke omgevingen.

TFC-aanpak: Behandel robotica als engineeringsysteem: besturing, betrouwbaarheid, kennis, software en menselijke goedkeuring samen.

SW

Industriele software

Probleemgebied: Technical teams often work across fragile spreadsheets, disconnected files, and generic tools.

Engineeringwaarde: Purpose-built software can make engineering work clearer, more repeatable, and easier to review.

TFC-aanpak: Build focused systems around evidence, workflows, reports, permissions, data quality, and practical operations.

AU

Automation

Probleemgebied: Repeated engineering work is easy to perform inconsistently when knowledge stays informal.

Engineeringwaarde: Automation turns repeatable work into controlled digital workflows with review points.

TFC-aanpak: Automate the structure around work while keeping decisions, exceptions, and approvals visible.

EI

Engineering Intelligence

Probleemgebied: Engineering work depends on context, calculations, documents, decisions, and long-term knowledge.

Engineeringwaarde: Intelligence becomes useful when it connects technical evidence to workflows and decisions.

TFC-aanpak: Use AI for orchestration and reasoning, deterministic tools for calculations, and citations for review.

DE

Digitale engineering

Probleemgebied: Engineering delivery needs structured requirements, tasks, reports, readiness, and change history.

Engineeringwaarde: Digital engineering systems help teams move from scattered activity to accountable execution.

TFC-aanpak: Model projects, cases, reports, tasks, and lessons as durable engineering objects.

AR

Asset- en reliabilitytechnologie

Probleemgebied: Asset decisions need maintenance knowledge, operating context, risk, and evidence over time.

Engineeringwaarde: Reliability thinking improves how teams understand failures, readiness, and operational constraints.

TFC-aanpak: Connect evidence, lessons, reports, and engineering reasoning so reliability knowledge compounds.

KG

Engineeringkennissystemen

Probleemgebied: Knowledge is often trapped in old projects, documents, conversations, and individual memory.

Engineeringwaarde: Structured knowledge preserves standards, decisions, lessons, and capability across future work.

TFC-aanpak: Build searchable knowledge structures with source quality, relationships, and human review.

Flagshipvoorbeeld

Hoe de lijnen samenkomen in ProjecTile

ProjecTile combines engineering intelligence, industrial software, automation, digital engineering, and engineering knowledge systems into one Engineering Operating System. It is the named public platform; other TFC work remains technology in development, research, or internal capability.
