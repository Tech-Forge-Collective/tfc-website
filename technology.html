Engineering Intelligence

Beyond chat. Built for engineering.

ProjecTile is designed as an Engineering Operating System that connects knowledge, projects, specialists, reports, deterministic tools, and decisions into one focused workspace.

Engineering Director

Problem solved: Engineering requests often need routing, clarification, and review before work begins.

Engineering value: Coordinates specialists, assumptions, missing information, reports, and quality review.

How it works: AI supports triage and reasoning, while structured workflows preserve decisions and evidence.

Engineering Knowledge Graph

Problem solved: Sources, reports, decisions, and lessons are often disconnected.

Engineering value: Relationships make project context searchable and explainable.

How it works: Evidence objects connect documents, reports, workflows, knowledge packs, and capability records.

Engineering DNA

Problem solved: Teams lose lessons after projects close.

Engineering value: Reusable decisions, workflows, templates, and lessons improve future work.

How it works: Completed work can be promoted into durable organisational knowledge.

Engineering Readiness Engine

Problem solved: Feature existence is not the same as reliable capability.

Engineering value: Readiness scores expose implementation, tests, evidence, documentation, and production validation.

How it works: Objective evidence contributes to readiness without manually inflating scores.

Knowledge Packs

Problem solved: Engineering knowledge is hard to package and reuse.

Engineering value: Packs group documents, templates, calculators, standards metadata, and specialist mappings.

How it works: AI can recommend packs, but validation and source quality remain explicit.

Engineering Toolboxes

Problem solved: Probabilistic answers should not replace deterministic calculations.

Engineering value: Calculators preserve formulas, units, assumptions, validation examples, and report outputs.

How it works: Specialists orchestrate deterministic tools instead of manually inventing calculations.

Engineering Reports

Problem solved: Engineering outputs need reviewable deliverables, not raw chat transcripts.

Engineering value: Reports include inputs, assumptions, calculations, risks, recommendations, QA review, and sources.

How it works: Technical writing formats structured specialist work into readable deliverables.

AI Provider Marketplace

Problem solved: Different engineering tasks need different model strengths, cost profiles, and reliability levels.

Engineering value: Provider routing can support task-specific orchestration without locking the platform to one model.

How it works: AI assists selection and reasoning, while deterministic tools and QA constrain engineering outputs.

Identity Service

Problem solved: Engineering assistants need consistent purpose, boundaries, and operating principles.

Engineering value: Identity keeps the platform aligned with engineering-first behaviour and human oversight.

How it works: System-level identity guides interaction without replacing formal engineering governance.
